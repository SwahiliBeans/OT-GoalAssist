Welcome to the OT GoalAssist Home Page!

OT GoalAssist proposes a software-based solution to a specific part of the administrative burden experienced by occupational therapists: manually drafting individualized treatment goals and plans of care. By combining structured assessment input, rule-based templates, and optional AI-generated language, the application could help therapists produce clear and editable goal drafts more efficiently. Its safeguards would keep the therapist in control of the final documentation and make sure that generated suggestions are grounded in the provided information. Ultimately, OT GoalAssist aims to make documentation less burdensome without losing sight of what matters most: supporting occupational therapists and helping patients work toward meaningful everyday activities.

The Problem:

Occupational therapists (OTs) help patients develop and maintain the skills they need for everyday activities. However, providing care involves more than simply working directly with patients. OTs must also evaluate patients, administer standardized assessments, develop individualized treatment plans, write measurable goals, and document progress. One particularly time-consuming task for OTs is writing treatment goals for each patient. These goals must be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant to the patient's needs, and appropriate for the expected treatment period. Therapists use a variety of frameworks to write these goals, including COAST, which organizes goals around the client, occupation, assistance level, specific conditions, and time frame. Documentation becomes an even greater challenge when healthcare organizations measure productivity by comparing billable time spent with patients against unbillable time spent on documentation. When productivity expectations are too high, therapists may not have enough scheduled time to complete their paperwork. This can contribute to stress, burnout, and less time available for patient care.

The Solution:

OT GoalAssist is a software application that helps occupational therapists craft treatment goals and plans of care using patient information, standardized assessment results, functional challenges, and patient or family priorities. Instead of writing every goal from scratch, a therapist could enter relevant findings from assessments such as the Bruininks-Oseretsky Test of Motor Proficiency (BOT-2), Peabody Developmental Motor Scales (PDMS), or manual muscle testing (MMT). The application would combine this information with the patient's functional needs to generate editable, COAST-formatted goal suggestions. The system would use rule-based templates and potentially an AI language model to produce readable, structured drafts. It would also identify missing information, such as an unspecified assistance level or time frame, so the therapist can complete the goal before using it. OT GoalAssist would assist therapists rather than replace their clinical judgment. Therapists would review and revise every generated goal and plan. The goal is to reduce repetitive administrative work while preserving individualized, patient-centered care.
